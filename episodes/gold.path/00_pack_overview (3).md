# AeroVista Agent ByteCast Training Pack — Days 8–10

## Purpose

This package contains the third reference-doc set for the AeroVista Agent ByteCast onboarding sequence.

Days 8–10 move agents from writing discipline into delivery discipline and final trusted-agent readiness.

## Included Days

| Day | Title | Core Skill |
|---:|---|---|
| 8 | Writing Like AeroVista: Clear, Useful, and Evidence-Based | Produce clear, useful, audience-aware writing grounded in evidence and protected by boundaries. |
| 9 | From Draft to Deliverable: Finishing the Slice | Define done, prevent scope creep, verify acceptance criteria, and ship usable slices. |
| 10 | Operating as a Trusted Agent: Judgment, Boundaries, and Momentum | Capstone readiness standard before supervised live AeroVista work. |

## Recommended Use

Use these documents as:

- ByteCast production scripts
- Agent training reference material
- Completion drill templates
- Internal onboarding material
- Basis for HTML/audio training modules
- Required training checkpoint before supervised live work

## Packaging Note

These are reference documents only. They are drafted training assets and are not yet packaged as published audio episodes, HTML training pages, or completion-tracking modules unless separately implemented.
