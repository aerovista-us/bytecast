## Training Arc

### Day 8 — Writing Like AeroVista
Agents learn how to produce docs, briefs, scripts, customer messages, and operational notes that are clear, useful, audience-aware, evidence-based, and safe.

### Day 9 — From Draft to Deliverable
Agents learn how to finish usable slices by defining done, avoiding scope creep, polishing in the right order, verifying acceptance criteria, and packaging work cleanly.

### Day 10 — Operating as a Trusted Agent
Agents complete the capstone readiness gate: judgment, boundaries, momentum, source discipline, queue hygiene, access awareness, useful writing, delivery readiness, and handoff quality.
